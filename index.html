SAP Login & Navigation Window Practice alongside the topic
🖥️

SAP GUI (SAP Logon) is a Windows program, not a webpage — this course can't display it inside this window, the same way it can't display Excel or Outlook.

  1. Open SAP Logon from your Windows Start Menu (or wherever your IT team installed it) and connect to your system as you normally do.
  2. Snap it beside this browser: drag its title bar to the left or right edge of your screen, or press ⊞ Win + ← / →. Do the same with this browser window on the other side.
  3. Follow each step in the course on one side while you repeat it in SAP GUI on the other.

Using two monitors? Just put SAP Logon on one screen and this browser on the other — no snapping needed.
